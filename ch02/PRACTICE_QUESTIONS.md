# Chapter 2: Flow Controls
## Conceptual Review Questions

### Question 10
What can you press if your program is stuck in an infinite loop?

*ANSWER:* 
CTRL + C

### QUESTION 11
What is the difference between break and continue?

*ANSWER:*
Break is the loop terminator and continue is the loop reinstater, which starts at the top of the loop again

### QUESTION 12
What is the difference between range(10), range(0, 10), and range(0, 10, 1)
in a for loop?

*ANSWER:*

1.) The incrementor starts from zero
2.) We pick where the incrementor starts and stops from
3.) We pick the start, stop, and increment amount

### QUESTION 14
If you had a function named bacon() inside a module named spam, how
would you call it after importing spam?

*ANSWER:*
spam.bacon()
